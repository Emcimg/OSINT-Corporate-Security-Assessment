# Defensive recommendations

The following themes summarize the recommendations in the source assessment without disclosing target-specific remediation details.

## Immediate priority
- Review externally accessible administrative services.
- Confirm that only necessary services and environments are exposed.
- Review legacy, test and auxiliary environments.
- Check mail and file-transfer configurations for secure transport and authentication.

## High priority
- Review corporate identity and authentication controls.
- Strengthen multi-factor authentication and credential hygiene.
- Review cloud and business-platform access permissions.
- Validate email security configuration and monitoring.

## Medium priority
- Review domain, DNS and certificate-management practices.
- Maintain web platforms, plugins and supporting components.
- Remove unnecessary metadata from publicly distributed documents.
- Provide ongoing awareness training on phishing and impersonation.

## Ongoing governance
- Maintain an inventory of internet-facing assets.
- Assign owners to remediation actions.
- Track remediation status and evidence of closure.
- Repeat the exposure review periodically and after significant infrastructure changes.

These are general defensive themes derived from the source report. They are not a substitute for validation by the organization's authorized technical team.
