# Methodology

## Scope and approach

The source assessment examined public information associated with an organization, its visible digital presence and its externally observable technical footprint. The source report states that the work was authorized and that collection was passive, without direct interaction with the organization's systems.

This public summary intentionally excludes the original scope identifiers, target domains, personal details and technical indicators.

## Intelligence cycle

### 1. Planning and direction
Establish the intelligence requirements, objectives, permitted scope, constraints and expected deliverables.

### 2. Collection
Gather relevant information from open sources, including public records, domain and certificate information, professional and social platforms, search engines and public technical repositories.

### 3. Processing
Deduplicate, structure and classify collected material. Record source context and distinguish raw observations from assumptions.

### 4. Analysis
Correlate information across sources, assess reliability and relevance, and consider how exposed information could contribute to security risks such as impersonation, phishing or discovery of externally visible services.

### 5. Dissemination
Present findings in a clear report, prioritize defensive actions and communicate limitations.

## Analytical principles

- Stay within the authorized scope.
- Prefer passive collection.
- Validate findings where possible and record uncertainty.
- Minimize personal data.
- Separate observed facts from analytical judgments.
- Do not publish operational indicators or sensitive evidence.
